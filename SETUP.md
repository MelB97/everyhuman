# Every Human website: setup and day-to-day

## Going live (one-off)
1. Connect this repo to Netlify (same as Clean Scapes and Every Business Solutions).
2. In Netlify, go to **Forms → Form notifications** and add an email notification for each form:
   - `enquiry` → admin@everyhuman.net.au
   - `accounts` → accounts@everyhuman.net.au
   - `careers` → admin@everyhuman.net.au
   - `team-update` → Mel and Kaileigh
3. Point everyhuman.net.au at Netlify.
4. Before launch, fill in the real phone number in **Business details**. The phone number stays hidden on the site until a real number is entered.

## Referrals
The referral page uses the Astalty form, so referrals go straight into Astalty, not by email. If Astalty gives you a new embed code, paste the web address from it into **Business details → Astalty referral form link**.

## Team changes
- **Staff (e.g. Hannah):** bookmark `everyhuman.net.au/staff/team-update/` and use that form. It isn't linked anywhere on the site and Google is told not to list it.
- **Mel or Kaileigh:** when a request arrives by email, open Pages CMS → **Our team**, then add, edit or delete the person. To hide someone without deleting them, untick **Show on the website**. No photo? Their initials are shown instead.

## Other edits
Everything else (services, locations, homepage wording, business details) is in Pages CMS too.
