We focus on doing things *with* you, not *for* you. Small steps add up, and we celebrate every one of them.

## Skills we can work on together

- Planning meals, shopping and cooking
- Budgeting and managing money
- Building daily routines and keeping on top of appointments
- Catching public transport and getting around safely
- Looking after your home
- Social skills and building friendships

## Children, teens and families

We also partner with children and their families, and follow the Child Safe Scheme and the National Principles for Child Safe Organisations.
