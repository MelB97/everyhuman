## Where we can take you

- Medical, allied health and other appointments
- Shopping and errands
- Work, study, day programs and activities
- Visiting family and friends

## Wheelchair accessible van

We have a wheelchair accessible van, so you can travel in your own chair, comfortably and safely.

All our drivers hold a current licence and drive registered, insured vehicles.
