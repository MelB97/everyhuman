## How we can help

- Personal care, like showering and dressing
- Cleaning, laundry and meal preparation
- Transport to appointments and shopping
- Social outings and company
- Medication prompts and reminders

## Funding

You can receive support through My Aged Care, or pay privately with no long contracts. Not sure where to start? Give us a call and we'll talk you through your options.
