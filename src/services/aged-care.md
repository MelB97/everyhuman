## How we can help

- Personal care, like showering and dressing
- Cleaning, laundry and meal preparation
- Transport to appointments and shopping
- Social outings and company
- Medication prompts and reminders

## Funding

We aren't a registered My Aged Care provider. Instead, we provide aged care support through **brokerage**: if you already receive aged care services through a provider, they can engage us to deliver your support, and it's paid from your existing funding.

Just ask your aged care provider or case manager to get in touch with us, or call us and we'll help you start the conversation. You can also pay privately, with no long contracts.
