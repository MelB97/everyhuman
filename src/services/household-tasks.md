We can help as much or as little as you need, and we'll work alongside you if you'd like to keep doing some things yourself.

## What we can help with

- General cleaning, vacuuming and mopping
- Laundry, ironing and changing linen
- Meal preparation
- Putting away groceries and tidying up
- Keeping walkways clear and your home safe
