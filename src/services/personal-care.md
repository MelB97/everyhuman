Personal care is personal, so we take the time to learn how you like things done and who you feel comfortable with. Your support worker follows your routine, not the other way around.

## What we can help with

- Showering, bathing and toileting
- Dressing and getting ready for the day or for bed
- Hair care, shaving and grooming
- Medication prompts and reminders
- Help getting in and out of bed or moving around your home
- Higher-needs personal care, delivered by trained support workers

## How we do it

- **Same faces where we can.** We aim to keep a small, consistent team around you.
- **Your say, always.** You choose what you need help with and how.
- **Privacy and dignity first.** Every support worker is screened, trained and treats your home with respect.
