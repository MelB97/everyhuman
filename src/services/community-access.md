Whether it's a coffee down the road, a weekly class or finally trying that hobby, your support worker is there to make it happen and make it fun.

## Ideas to get you started

- Shopping, banking and running errands
- Sport, the gym, swimming or a walk on the beach
- Art, music, craft and other classes
- Local events, markets, the library or the movies
- Catching up with friends and family
- Volunteering or building skills for work

## Built around your goals

We'll look at the goals in your plan and find activities that help you work towards them, while doing the things you actually enjoy.
