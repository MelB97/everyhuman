{{ site.business_name }} Pty Ltd (ABN {{ site.abn | abn }}) ("we", "us") provides in-home and community support in New South Wales and Queensland to NDIS participants, older Australians and private clients. We respect your right to privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we keep it safe, and what you can do if you have a question or concern.

We follow the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles, the Health Records and Information Privacy Act 2002 (NSW) for health information we hold in New South Wales, and the privacy requirements of the NDIS Practice Standards and NDIS Code of Conduct.

## The information we collect

**Clients and the people who support them.** To provide safe, good-quality supports, we collect information such as:

- your name, date of birth, address, phone number and email
- your NDIS number, plan, funding and plan manager details, or your aged care or private funding details
- information about your disability, health, medications, support needs, goals and preferences
- your cultural background, language and communication needs, if you choose to tell us
- details of your family, guardian, nominee, support coordinator, emergency contacts and other people involved in your supports
- notes, incident reports, feedback and complaints about the supports we provide
- photos or videos, but only with your consent

Some of this is "sensitive information" under the Privacy Act, such as health information. We only collect it with your consent, or where the law allows or requires it.

**Staff, contractors and job applicants.** We collect details such as contact details, resumes, qualifications, references, worker screening and Working with Children Check clearances, and the information we need to pay and employ people.

**Website visitors, referrers and other contacts.** We collect the details you give us when you send an enquiry, make a referral or apply for a job, such as names, contact details, suburb, funding type and the supports needed.

## How we collect it

We collect most information directly from you, or from someone you've asked to act for you. With your consent, we may also collect information from your support coordinator, plan manager, the NDIA, a registered aged care provider we deliver services for, your doctor or other health professionals, and people who refer you to us.

You can contact us without giving your name for general questions. To provide supports, we need to know who you are.

## Why we collect and use it

We use personal information to:

- respond to enquiries and referrals
- plan, provide, review and coordinate your supports
- keep you, our staff and others safe, and meet our duty of care
- send invoices and claims to the NDIA, your plan manager, an aged care provider or you
- meet our legal and registration obligations, such as reporting incidents, managing complaints and taking part in audits
- recruit, train, roster and pay our staff
- improve the quality of our services

We don't sell or rent personal information. We won't use your photos or stories in our marketing without your consent, and you can withdraw that consent at any time.

## Who we share it with

We only share personal information when it's needed for the purpose it was collected for, when you agree, or when the law requires or allows it. This may include:

- people you've asked us to involve, such as family, a guardian, nominee or support coordinator
- the NDIA, your plan manager, or the registered aged care provider we deliver services for, to arrange and pay for supports
- doctors, hospitals or emergency services when needed to keep you safe
- regulators and authorities when the law requires it, such as the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission, the Aged Care Quality and Safety Commission, child protection agencies, the Queensland Family and Child Commission, the NSW Office of the Children's Guardian, or the police
- independent auditors who check that we meet the NDIS Practice Standards (they must keep your information confidential, and you can choose whether to take part in an audit yourself)
- the service providers who help us run our business, such as our client management system, IT and email providers

## How we store and protect it

We keep client records, forms and registers in **Astalty**, a secure care management system. Astalty stores data in Australia. We also use Google Workspace for email and documents, and our website forms are processed by our website host, Netlify.

We protect information with password-protected systems, access limited to the staff who need it, staff training, confidentiality agreements and locked storage for any paper records. We keep records for as long as the law requires, usually 7 years, and then securely destroy or de-identify them.

## Information held overseas

Our client records in Astalty stay in Australia. Some of our other service providers, including Google and Netlify, may store or process information on servers outside Australia, including in the United States. We use well-known providers with strong security and take reasonable steps to make sure they protect your information.

## Decisions made by computer programs

We don't use computer programs to make decisions about you that could significantly affect your rights or interests. Decisions about your supports are made by people.

## Our website

Website forms are stored by Netlify and sent to our business email. Please don't send NDIS plans, medical information or bank details through the website. We'll collect anything like that securely once we're in contact. Our website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

## Accessing and correcting your information

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it. Just contact us using the details below. We'll respond within 30 days. There's no charge. If we can't give you access, we'll explain why in writing.

## Data breaches

If your information is lost or accessed without permission, and this is likely to cause you serious harm, we will tell you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, and take steps to reduce the harm. This is required under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.
