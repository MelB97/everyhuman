This policy explains how {{ site.business_name }} (ABN {{ site.abn | abn }}) ("we", "us") handles personal information collected through this website.

## What we collect

When you send an enquiry, referral or job application through our website, we collect the details you provide, such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, suburbs, funding type, the supports needed and anything else you choose to tell us. Job applicants may also attach a resume.

Please don't send NDIS plans, medical information or bank details through the website. We'll collect anything like that securely once we're in contact.

## How we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry, referral or application, and to provide or arrange services. We don't sell or rent your personal information.

## How it's stored

Forms sent through this website are processed and stored by our website host, Netlify, which may store data on servers outside Australia, and are forwarded to our business email. We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure and delete website submissions once they're no longer needed.

## Client information

Information about clients and participants is handled under our obligations as a registered NDIS provider and under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), and is not collected through this website.

## Cookies

This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

## Access, corrections and complaints

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, ask us to delete it, or make a privacy complaint, by emailing {{ site.email }}{% if site.phone | hasPhone %} or calling {{ site.phone }}{% endif %}. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always on this page.
