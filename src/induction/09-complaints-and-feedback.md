Every Human welcomes feedback and complaints. Participants have the right to raise concerns about their supports and services and should feel safe to speak up when something is not right.

A complaint may be made by a participant, family member, representative, advocate or another person.

Complaints may relate to Every Human, a Support Worker, the quality of support provided, communication, safety, privacy, behaviour, or any other concern about the service.

### If a Participant Raises a Complaint With You

If a participant tells you they are unhappy or wants to make a complaint:

- listen calmly and respectfully;
- take the concern seriously;
- allow them time to explain what has happened;
- do not become defensive, argue or try to talk them out of complaining;
- do not make promises about what the outcome will be;
- explain that you can support them to raise the concern with Every Human;
- report the complaint or concern to your Area Manager; and
- document the complaint as required.

Never discourage a participant from making a complaint because you believe the issue is minor or can be dealt with informally.

### If the Complaint Is About You

Remain professional.

Listen to the participant and do not argue, justify your actions or pressure them to withdraw the complaint.

Advise your Area Manager and allow Every Human's complaints process to be followed.

A participant has the right to raise concerns about their support, including about the Support Worker providing that support.

### Supporting Participants to Make a Complaint

Some participants may need assistance to communicate or make a complaint.

Support them to use their preferred communication method and provide assistance where appropriate without changing their words or influencing what they say.

Participants may also choose to have a family member, representative, advocate or other trusted person assist them.

### No Disadvantage for Speaking Up

A participant must not be treated differently, threatened, intimidated or disadvantaged because they have made a complaint or provided negative feedback.

Complaints and feedback can help Every Human identify problems and improve the quality and safety of our supports.

### Where to Find the Complaints Process

The Every Human Complaints & Feedback Management Policy and Complaint and Feedback Form are available through the Every Human Staff Portal.

If you are unsure what to do when a complaint is raised, contact your Area Manager for guidance.

Remember: Take it seriously — Support them — Report it — Document it.
