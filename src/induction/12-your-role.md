Being a Support Worker at Every Human is about more than completing a list of tasks.

Good support starts with knowing the participant you are supporting and understanding what is important to them.

Before providing support, you should understand the participant's:

- routines and preferences;
- communication needs;
- goals and interests;
- support needs;
- relevant health and safety information;
- risks and participant-specific instructions; and
- plans or procedures relevant to your role.

### Supporting Goals and Everyday Life

Support should reflect what matters to the participant.

Depending on the participant and their support arrangements, your role may include assisting with everyday activities, personal care, household tasks, appointments, community access, relationships, interests, skill development or other goals.

Look beyond simply completing the immediate task. Pay attention to what the participant wants from their support and what is important to them.

### Use Initiative and Stay Engaged

We expect Support Workers to use appropriate initiative.

This does not mean making decisions for the participant or changing their support arrangements. It means being observant, engaged and willing to respond appropriately to what is happening during the shift.

If planned tasks are completed, do not automatically disengage or spend the remainder of the shift on your phone.

Consider whether the participant would like support with something else that is appropriate to their goals, interests, routines or everyday life.

Being engaged does not mean constantly directing, entertaining or pressuring a participant to do activities. Some participants may prefer quiet time, conversation, company or simply knowing support is available.

### Work Within Your Role

Only provide support that is within your role, training and competence.

Do not:

- change a participant's support plan or instructions yourself;
- make clinical decisions outside your role;
- provide support you have not been trained or authorised to provide;
- make promises or commitments on behalf of Every Human; or
- guess what to do when you are unsure.

If something is unclear, outside your role, or you believe additional support or guidance is required, contact Every Human.

### Good Support Is Individual

There is no single way to be a good Support Worker because every participant is different.

What works well for one participant may be completely inappropriate for another.

Get to know the person. Follow their individual information. Listen. Observe. Ask when you are unsure.

At the end of a shift, good support should reflect what mattered to the participant - not simply what tasks were completed.
