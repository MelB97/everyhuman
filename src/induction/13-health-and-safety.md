Every Human is committed to providing supports that are safe for participants, workers and other people involved in our services.

Support Workers also have a responsibility to take reasonable care of their own health and safety and the health and safety of others while working.

### Before and During a Shift

Be aware of the environment you are working in and any participant-specific risks or safety instructions relevant to your role.

This may include:

- hazards in the participant's home or community
- mobility and manual handling risks
- infection risks
- animals or other people in the environment
- equipment or vehicles being used
- behaviours or situations identified in participant-specific plans
- environmental conditions such as heat, weather or unsafe access.

Do not assume that because you have worked somewhere before, the environment will always be the same.

### If You Identify a Hazard

If you notice something that could cause harm:

- Take reasonable action to make the immediate situation safer, if you can do so safely.
- Do not place yourself or another person at unnecessary risk.
- Inform the participant about the hazard where appropriate, particularly if it may affect their safety or support.
- Report the hazard or concern to Every Human.
- Document or complete any required report.
- A hazard should not simply be left for the next worker to discover.

### Incidents and Injuries

If an incident or injury occurs:

- attend to immediate safety
- call emergency services if required
- follow participant-specific emergency plans where relevant
- contact your Area Manager as soon as practicable
- complete required documentation and incident reporting.

Do not minimise an incident because nobody appears to have been injured. Some incidents and near misses still need to be reported so risks can be addressed.

### Manual Handling

Do not attempt a transfer, lift or other manual handling task that you are not trained, competent or authorised to perform.

Follow the participant's current manual handling instructions and use required equipment correctly.

If equipment is unavailable, damaged or does not appear safe, do not improvise a different transfer method. Contact Every Human for guidance.

### Your Safety Comes First

Your safety is Every Human's first priority.

You are never expected to remain in a situation where you feel unsafe.

If at any time during a shift you feel unsafe because of a person, behaviour, environment, animal, situation or any other concern:

- leave the immediate situation and move to a safe place
- do not put yourself at risk trying to manage the situation alone
- contact your Area Manager as soon as you are safe
- call 000 if there is an immediate threat or emergency
- follow any further instructions provided by Every Human
- document and report the incident as required.

You do not need to wait until someone is injured or until a situation becomes an emergency before leaving.

If something does not feel safe, trust that concern. Get yourself to safety first, then contact us.

No Support Worker will be expected to remain in an unsafe situation simply because they are rostered to provide support.

### Fitness for Work

You must be fit to safely perform your duties.

If illness, injury, fatigue, medication or another issue may affect your ability to safely provide support, contact your Area Manager.

### Remember

Notice it. Make it safe where you reasonably can. Report it.

Do not ignore hazards, assume someone else will deal with them, or improvise when you are not trained to manage the situation.
