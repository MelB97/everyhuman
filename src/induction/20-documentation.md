Accurate documentation is an important part of safe, professional support.

Every Human uses Astalty as part of the official service record. Information entered into Astalty may later be reviewed by participants, families, coordinators, clinicians, auditors or the NDIS Commission.

### Shift Notes

Shift Notes are the permanent record of the support provided.

They should include:

- what support was provided
- relevant observations
- participant presentation and engagement
- progress towards goals
- appointments or activities attended
- health, behaviour or safety concerns
- refusals or changes in support
- incidents or follow-up required
- anything important that may help explain patterns over time.

Shift Notes should be completed accurately, objectively and within the required timeframe.

### Write What You Observed

Document facts, not assumptions.

Instead of - “Sam was aggressive.”

Write - “Sam raised his voice, hit the table with an open hand and said, ‘Leave me alone.’”

Instead of - “Mary was depressed.”

Write - “Mary spoke very little during the shift, remained in bed until 11am and stated that she did not feel like going out today.”

Describe what you saw, heard or were told. Do not diagnose.

Do not include personal opinions, judgemental language, gossip or information that is not relevant to the participant's support.

If you did not see or hear something yourself, make it clear where the information came from. For example: “Participant stated...” or “Family member advised...”

### Documentation Does Not Replace Reporting

Recording something in Shift Notes does not replace the need to tell your Area Manager when an issue requires immediate attention or follow-up.

Significant health changes, medication issues, incidents, safeguarding concerns, safety risks or other urgent matters must be reported through the appropriate process as well as documented.

Do not assume that because something has been written in your Shift Notes, management has seen it immediately.

### Avoid Vague Notes

Notes such as:

- “Good shift.”
- “All okay.”
- “Went shopping.”
- “Client was fine.”

do not provide enough information.

Your notes should show what actually happened during the shift and anything relevant to the participant’s support, goals or wellbeing.

### Handover

Handover is for information the next worker genuinely needs to know.

This may include:

- a change in routine
- an upcoming appointment
- medication or equipment information
- something that needs to be followed up
- a safety or access issue
- important information relevant to the next shift.

Handover does not replace Shift Notes.

Important health, behaviour, incident or support information must still be recorded in the appropriate permanent record.

Keep handover relevant, factual and limited to information the next worker needs to provide safe and appropriate support.

### Team Chat

Team Chat is for day-to-day operational communication.

It is not the participant's clinical or permanent service record.

Important information that workers need to be aware of may be pinned in Team Chat so it remains easy for the team to find. Workers should check pinned posts relevant to the participant they are supporting.

Workers are not expected to monitor Team Chat when they are off duty. When returning to work, check for relevant updates and pinned information before providing support.

Do not rely on Team Chat alone to record:

- incidents
- medication issues
- significant health changes
- behaviours of concern
- participant disclosures
- important changes in support.

These must also be documented in the appropriate permanent record and, where required, reported directly to your Area Manager.

If information is important for the team to continue referring to, it may be pinned — but pinning a post does not replace Shift Notes, incident reporting or other required documentation.

### Confidentiality

Do not discuss participant information in group chats or with workers who do not need the information for their role.

Keep documentation professional and respectful.

### Remember

If it matters to the participant’s support, safety, wellbeing or progress, it should be documented properly.

Shift Notes = permanent record

Handover = what the next worker needs

Team Chat = operational communication
