As an Every Human Support Worker, you are expected to provide support that is safe, respectful, professional and person-centred.

Every Human workers must follow the NDIS Code of Conduct when providing NDIS supports and services.

### What this means in your everyday work

You are expected to:

- Respect individual rights — support each person's privacy, dignity, independence, choice and control.
- Treat people with respect — communicate respectfully and never humiliate, intimidate, discriminate against or deliberately exclude a participant.
- Provide support safely and competently — work within your role, training and experience, and ask for guidance when you are unsure.
- Act honestly and with integrity — maintain professional boundaries and never take advantage of a participant or your position as a Support Worker.
- Raise concerns — promptly report concerns that may affect the quality or safety of a participant's support.
- Prevent and respond to harm — take reasonable steps to prevent and respond to violence, abuse, neglect, exploitation and sexual misconduct.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality — only access, use or share participant information when required for your role and through appropriate Every Human processes.

### If You Are Unsure

You are not expected to know everything.

You are expected to recognise when you are unsure and seek guidance.

If you are asked to do something that you believe may be unsafe, outside your role, inconsistent with a participant's plan or instructions, or contrary to the Code of Conduct, do not simply proceed because someone has asked you to.

Contact Every Human for guidance.

### Professional Conduct at Every Human

The Code of Conduct applies throughout your work — including in a participant's home, in the community, while transporting participants, when communicating with families and other providers, and when documenting or discussing participant information.

Your conduct should always protect the participant's rights, dignity, safety and support their choice and control.

### Required Reading

Please read the [NDIS Code of Conduct](https://www.ndiscommission.gov.au/rules-and-standards/codes-conduct) on the NDIS Commission website.

You are responsible for reading, understanding and following the Code while providing NDIS supports through Every Human.

There are 7 essential elements in the Code of Conduct. They are:

1. Respect for individuals' rights
2. Respect for individuals' privacy
3. Provide support safely with competence, care and skill
4. Act with integrity, honesty and transparency
5. Raise and act on concerns relating to quality and safety of support
6. Take steps to prevent and respond to abuse, violence and neglect
7. Take steps to prevent and respond to sexual misconduct

The NDIS Code of Conduct and Every Human's Code of Conduct Policy are also available in Astalty, under **Profile → Resources**.
