Every Human provides support at home to older people receiving aged care services. This section explains how that works and the rules that apply.

### How we provide aged care

We are not a registered My Aged Care provider. We provide aged care support through **brokerage**: a client's registered aged care provider engages Every Human to deliver some of their services.

This means:

- the client's aged care provider remains responsible for their care and their care plan;
- you must follow the client's care plan and any instructions from their provider, as well as Every Human's procedures;
- you must not change, add to or stop any part of the client's support yourself; and
- any changes, concerns or requests go through Every Human, so we can raise them with the client's provider.

If a client asks for something outside their care plan, explain that you need to check, and contact your Area Manager.

### The new Aged Care Act

A new Aged Care Act started on 1 November 2025. It puts the rights of older people at the centre of their care.

Every older person receiving aged care has rights under the **Statement of Rights**, including the right to:

- make their own decisions about their life and care, with support if they want it;
- be treated with dignity and respect, and have their culture, identity and life experience valued;
- receive safe, high quality care;
- have their privacy respected;
- stay connected with family, friends and their community; and
- raise concerns or make a complaint without fear of it affecting their care.

### The Aged Care Code of Conduct

When you provide aged care support, you must follow the **Aged Care Code of Conduct**. It is very similar to the NDIS Code of Conduct. You must:

1. act with respect for people's rights to freedom of expression, self-determination and decision-making, in line with applicable laws;
2. act in a way that treats people with dignity and respect, and values their diversity;
3. act with respect for people's privacy;
4. provide care, supports and services safely and competently, with care and skill;
5. act with integrity, honesty and transparency;
6. promptly take steps to raise and act on concerns about matters that may affect the quality and safety of care;
7. provide care, supports and services free from all forms of violence, discrimination, exploitation, neglect and abuse; and
8. take all reasonable steps to prevent and respond to all forms of sexual misconduct.

### Serious incidents in aged care

Aged care has its own reporting scheme, the **Serious Incident Response Scheme (SIRS)**. Serious incidents include:

- unreasonable use of force;
- unlawful sexual contact or inappropriate sexual conduct;
- psychological or emotional abuse;
- an unexpected death;
- stealing or financial coercion by a staff member;
- neglect;
- inappropriate use of restrictive practices; and
- a client going missing unexpectedly.

Some serious incidents must be reported to the Aged Care Quality and Safety Commission within 24 hours, so speed matters.

**Your role:** if you see, suspect or are told about a serious incident, make sure everyone is safe, call 000 in an emergency, and report it to your Area Manager **immediately**. Every Human will notify the client's aged care provider so the incident can be reported correctly. Record what you actually saw, heard or were told.

You do not need to decide whether something is a serious incident. If you are unsure, report it.

### Help and advice for older people

You can share these free services with clients and their families:

- **Aged Care Quality and Safety Commission** (complaints): [1800 951 822](tel:1800951822)
- **Older Persons Advocacy Network (OPAN)**: [1800 700 600](tel:1800700600)
- **Elder abuse phone line (1800ELDERHelp)**: [1800 353 374](tel:1800353374)

### Remember

Follow the care plan. Respect the person's rights. Report concerns to your Area Manager straight away. Every Human will speak with the client's aged care provider.
