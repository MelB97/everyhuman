Welcome to Every Human. We are pleased to have you joining our team.

At Every Human, we believe great support starts with seeing the person first.

Every person we support is different. They have their own personality, relationships, routines, interests, abilities, goals and ideas about how they want to live their life. Our role is not to take over - it is to provide the right support, in the right way, for that individual.

### What We Expect From Our Support Workers

We want Support Workers who are kind, reliable, observant and willing to take initiative.

Good support is more than turning up and completing a list of tasks. We expect you to get to know the people you support, listen to them, respect their choices and look for opportunities to support their independence, confidence, relationships, interests and goals.

We also expect you to recognise when something doesn't look right and to ask rather than guess.

### What You Can Expect From Us

We don't expect you to know everything.

We want our staff to feel comfortable asking questions, raising concerns, requesting guidance and telling us when they need support.

If you make a mistake, something goes wrong, or you are unsure what to do, tell us. Raising something early gives us the opportunity to support you, address the situation and keep everyone safe.

Your safety matters too. You will never be expected to place yourself at risk simply because you are at work.

### Working Together

The best support happens when participants, Support Workers, families where appropriate, management and other professionals communicate and work together.

Throughout this induction, you will learn how Every Human works, what we expect from you and where to find the information and support you need to do your job well.

Welcome to the team. We are here to support you, too.
